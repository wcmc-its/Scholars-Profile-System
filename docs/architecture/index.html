System context

The one-glance picture for newcomers and stakeholders: every displayed value is derived — 20+ upstream connectors feed a scheduled ETL into the platform's stores, which the app serves to the public and to authenticated staff editors. Headshots load live at read time straight from the WCM directory (directory.weill.cornell.edu) — never stored, never via the ETL. On-demand sources (RePORTER, NSF) aren't on a Step Functions cadence yet; they run via the daily prototype chain. Human Resources (employer/employee mentees) is a planned source, not yet built. POPS (the public weillcornell.org physician directory) enriches clinical scholars with board certifications, specialties, and expertise (etl/pops/index.ts) — it runs after Enterprise Directory (it keys off the clinical-profile flag ED sets) and feeds the people search index; it rides the daily chain, not yet a standalone Step Functions step. Clinical trials originate in OnCore (the CTMS — a manual institutional export, static until the next export lands) and are enriched against the ClinicalTrials.gov registry (API v2); both stage into reciterdb tables (clinical_trials / clinical_trials_enriched, the latter pulled upstream by ReciterAI) that the nightly ETL reads — SPS never calls ClinicalTrials.gov directly. CTL portfolio (available technologies) is WCM's own Center for Technology Licensing, scraped weekly from its public portal (innovation.weill.cornell.edu). WCM Newsroom is read weekly for articles that mention a scholar (news.weill.cornell.edu/news/feed.json, etl/news/*) — one paginated JSON read covers the archive back to 1997. A vivo.weill.cornell.edu link in the article body publishes the mention outright; a prose name-match queues it for human review in /edit. The Research office's news page (research.weill.cornell.edu/about-us/news-updates) is a syndication target of the newsroom, not a source — SPS scraped it until #2200/#2231.

View 3 · for the cloud team

Network topology

The review-ready network picture: VPC, subnet tiers, the three default-deny security groups (SG-to-SG only), and VPC endpoints. The settled direction (2026-06-30 decision) is full consolidation: the two per-env Sps VPCs are replaced by one shared, TGW-attached its-reciter-vpc01 that hosts the whole estate — App + Data + ETL, both envs — with no VPC peering, env isolation by per-env security groups in one shared CIDR, and WCM / on-prem sources reached natively over the TGW (closing the #443 gap). The edge front door is NetScaler (View ④). Cutover is flag-gated and reversible — pending.

Settled direction — full consolidation (2026-06-30)

  • The two per-env Sps VPCs are replaced by one shared, TGW-attached its-reciter-vpc01; the whole estate (App + Data + ETL, both envs) moves in. No VPC peering.
  • Env isolation is by per-env security groups inside the one shared VPC — no network boundary, SG-to-SG only.
  • WCM / on-prem sources (ED-LDAP, ReciterDB, ASMS, COI, Jenzabar) are reached natively over the shared VPC's TGW attachment — this closes the #443 connectivity gap.
  • Edge front door = NetScaler (CloudFront + WAF → NetScaler → ALB → Fargate; View ④), inserted as a follow-on decoupled from this move.
  • Cutover is flag-gated and reversible (data moves by snapshot-restore into a fresh cluster alongside the live one) — pending. See docs/network-security-topology.md.

View 4 · edge topology — both envs live (#502)

Edge topology — NetScaler live in both environments

NetScaler is live in both environments (staging 2026-07-21, prod 2026-07-24). CloudFront sends all dynamic behaviours plus the default through the NetScaler VIP → app ALB: the origin leg is HTTPS-only (an HTTP origin behind the VIP's HTTP→HTTPS upgrade loops forever — the original ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS bug), and NetScaler dials the ALB on :443, forwarding the X-Origin-Verify header CloudFront injects. Durable in CDK via the #1507 origin-flip (PR #1852 staging, #1926 prod). Port corrected 2026-07-25 — this view previously said :80 and called the ALB :443 listener an unused guard; re-measurement (VPC flow logs + a timed causal probe) showed :443 is the live leg in both envs. The :443 TLS policy is now at parity — both envs carry the #1929 AEAD-only pin, staging deployed 2026-07-27 (#1937).

View 6 · the grant-matching subsystem at a glance

Grant matching — system context

How a funding opportunity travels from its source to a researcher's screen: ReciterAI's pipeline_grants fetches, judges, and persists the corpus into account-shared stores (DynamoDB reciterai + S3), SPS's nightly projection mirrors it into Aurora and the scholars-opportunities index, and the Grant Matcha surfaces serve it — with a staff intake loop feeding URLs back into the same pipeline, and a freshness alarm watching the whole supply chain.

Cadences: the ReciterAI ingest runs daily at 03:00 UTC (EventBridge → Fargate, reciterai-grants, shipped 2026-08-20 closing ReciterAI#269's gap); SPS projects at 07:00 UTC — a 4-hour launch gap, measured on first tick. SPIN is manual-only until the license question on scheduled bulk pulls clears (its weekly rule is spec'd but deliberately unshipped). WCM funding DB is frozen — its one-off crawler left no code; SPIN overlap is being measured before deciding rebuild vs retire. The submission drain is DynamoDB-only: it never publishes the S3 artifact (a per-run publish would trip the sweep's shrink guard). Suppression is a soft-delete the nightly upsert can never resurrect — the upsert's column list simply never names the three suppress* columns. The grants-for-me card rides SELF_EDIT_GRANT_RECS (staging-on, prod-off).

View 7 · supply chain + guardrails

Opportunity corpus pipeline & freshness

Inside the daily corpus run: every source funnels through one normalize → dedupe → gate → LLM judge → persist chain in the reciterai-grants Fargate task, with the SPS submission drain chained after it. Two independent guardrails watch it: a log-error alarm on the producer (the ; chain means a grants.gov failure never reaches the exit code) and the per-source freshness metric + ≥21-day alarm on the SPS side.

Why two alarms: the freshness metric's corpus-wide series goes green when any source lands a fresh row (a manual drain once masked three 45-60-day-stale feeds), so the per-source dimensions exist for per-feed alarms once each cadence proves out; the log-error alarm covers failures the exit code can't (the ; chain deliberately lets the drain run after a grants.gov crash). Drain is DynamoDB-only: on 2026-08-20 the old drain-side publish clobbered grants/latest with a 65-item subset — exactly what the shrink guard exists to refuse — so the drain no longer publishes at all; SPS reads the corpus from DynamoDB, never the artifact. SPIN runs manually until the license check on scheduled pulls clears (weekly rule spec'd, deliberately unshipped). The 03:00→07:00 UTC gap is a launch gap; first-tick wall-clock vs 07:00 is a monitored assumption, and Fargate has no task timeout — a hung run runs until noticed.

View 8 · who asks, which engine answers

Matching surfaces & engines

The three grant-matching surfaces and the engine each one rides: Grant Matcha's seeded ask and /edit/matcha's plain ask share the people spine (LLM extraction → cluster retrieval → RRF fuse), the dark grants target awaits a UI consumer, and the self-service card uses the no-LLM forward matcher over the opportunities index. Eligibility is deliberately split: “what a grant requires” is extracted upstream by ReciterAI; “does this researcher qualify” is the client-side rail — the single owner since the find-researchers sunset.

Retired 2026-08-20 (Phase 3): /edit/find-researchers and its structured reverse matcher (rankResearchersForOpportunity survives only for two measurement scripts); its abstain floor (GRANT_MATCHER_ABSTAIN_FLOOR) is deleted outright — the ask card's thin-match caution (from assessMatchSignal) is the surviving low-signal warning. Suppression semantics: browse/detail hide a suppressed opportunity immediately (Aurora reads); index-backed surfaces — both spines' retrieval and the forward matcher — lag by one nightly rebuild, stated in the suppress dialog. Cost shape: every spine ask bills Bedrock Sonnet (the task role is deliberately scoped to Opus/Sonnet only — there is no cheap-model tier), which is why the result cache and its Phase-2 durable upgrade exist. The grants spine serves target:“grants” behind GRANT_MATCHA with its own cache namespace; its result-card UI was deliberately reverted pending the convergence eval.
